Features

Everything an image library needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Built for IT teams, labs and homelabs tired of hunting for the right installer on a shared drive.

01 · Upload

Multi-gigabyte, resumable

Drag & drop files of any size. Chunked uploads survive network drops — or let the server import straight from a vendor URL.

02 · Integrity

Checksums you can trust

SHA-256 is computed in the background, compared with the vendor's hash and searchable. Duplicates are flagged automatically.

03 · Sharing

Secure share links

Password, expiration date and download limit. Get notified on every download.

04 · Access

SSO, LDAP & roles

Sign in with OpenID Connect or your LDAP / Active Directory. Groups, invitation-only sign-up and 2FA.

05 · Storage

Any backend

Local, NFS, S3-compatible, FTP and SFTP — credentials encrypted at rest.

06 · Speed

Command palette

Press ⌘K or / to jump anywhere or search the library.

07 · Audit

Full history

Every change recorded with its author, plus storage and usage statistics.

08 · Yours

Your brand

Light & dark themes, custom name, logo and accent color. English, French, Spanish, German.

Every image is presented like a product, with its own silver disc and the details that matter.

Single sign-on

Your identity provider. Your directory.

Connect Keycloak, Authentik, Microsoft Entra ID, Google, Okta or GitLab with OpenID Connect — or any OAuth 2 provider such as GitHub. Prefer passwords? Plug in LDAP or Active Directory. Accounts can be created on first sign-in and an IdP or directory group can grant the admin role. Local accounts and Silo's 2FA keep working.

Install

Up and running in three commands.

One container with everything included: FrankenPHP, Redis, the queue worker and the scheduler. Put it behind your reverse proxy with HTTPS.

  1. Download the compose file and the environment template

    curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pokertour/Silo/main/docker-compose.yml
curl -o .env https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pokertour/Silo/main/.env.example

  2. Fill in APP_KEY, APP_URL and the database passwords

    echo "APP_KEY=base64:$(openssl rand -base64 32)"
openssl rand -base64 32   # for DB_PASSWORD and DB_ROOT_PASSWORD

  3. Start Silo and create the administrator

    docker compose up -d
docker compose exec app php artisan db:seed --force

    Open http://localhost:8080 and sign in with the generated password.
